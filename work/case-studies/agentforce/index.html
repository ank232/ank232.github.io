Executive Summary

Yotrio US receives a large volume of warranty and product support requests through Email-to-Case from retail partners and customers. Processing these requests traditionally required support agents to manually analyse emails, identify the requester, collect missing information, validate warranty eligibility and perform the required business actions before responding. To modernise this workflow, I designed and implemented a production-ready multi-agent AI platform using Salesforce Agentforce. The solution automatically analyses incoming emails, classifies the sender, orchestrates specialised AI agents, gathers missing information through conversational interactions, validates warranty eligibility using enterprise knowledge, performs business operations and prepares contextual responses for support teams. Rather than replacing customer service representatives, the platform reduces repetitive operational work while ensuring every business decision remains grounded in company policies and trusted enterprise data. As the sole engineer on the project, I was responsible for the complete architecture, implementation, testing and deployment of the solution.

The Challenge

Yotrio's customer support organisation handles warranty requests from multiple retail vendors, distributors and end customers. Although every request arrived through Email-to-Case, processing each case required significant manual effort before an agent could even begin resolving the issue. Support representatives needed to determine who had sent the email, understand the customer's intent, identify the affected product, verify warranty eligibility, collect any missing information and finally perform the required business operation such as initiating a replacement order or escalating the request. The process relied heavily on human interpretation, resulting in repetitive work, inconsistent information gathering and longer response cycles whenever essential details were missing. The challenge was not simply answering customer questions using AI. The solution needed to understand business context, follow warranty policies, interact naturally with customers, execute Salesforce business processes and maintain enterprise-grade governance throughout every interaction.

The Solution

Instead of implementing a single conversational AI assistant, I designed a multi-agent architecture where specialised AI agents collaborate under a central orchestration layer. Every incoming email enters Salesforce through Email-to-Case before being analysed by an orchestration agent responsible for understanding the conversation, identifying the requester and selecting the appropriate specialist agent. Vendor enquiries and customer warranty requests follow different business paths. Dedicated AI agents handle these independently while sharing enterprise knowledge through Data Cloud grounded retrieval. When sufficient information is unavailable, the platform conducts structured conversations with the customer to collect only the required details before continuing the workflow. Once all mandatory information has been collected, the platform validates warranty rules, executes the appropriate Salesforce business operations and prepares an accurate draft response for the support team. This architecture separates business reasoning from business execution, making the platform easier to extend as additional support scenarios are introduced.

Architecture Overview

The architecture follows a layered orchestration model where each component performs a clearly defined responsibility. Incoming emails are received through Salesforce Email-to-Case and passed into the Agentforce orchestration layer. The Orchestrator Agent performs initial reasoning by analysing the conversation, identifying the sender and determining whether the request should be handled by the Vendor Agent or Customer Agent. Each specialist agent is responsible only for its business domain. Prompt Templates guide reasoning while Agentforce maintains conversational context across multiple email exchanges. Whenever product information, warranty policies or documentation is required, the agents retrieve grounded knowledge from Data Cloud instead of relying solely on language model reasoning. Business operations such as warranty validation, replacement order creation, case updates and draft email generation are executed using deterministic Salesforce automation implemented through Apex, Flows and ConnectApi integrations. This separation ensures AI performs reasoning while Salesforce remains responsible for authoritative business execution.

Technical Implementation

Phase 1 · Ingestion & Classification

Every support request begins with Salesforce Email-to-Case. An orchestration layer analyses the incoming conversation, extracts contextual information and determines whether the request originated from a retail vendor or an end customer. This initial classification establishes the execution path for the remainder of the workflow while preserving conversation history for future reasoning.

Phase 2 · Multi-Agent Delegation

Rather than embedding every business rule inside one large prompt, the solution delegates responsibility to specialised AI agents. The Orchestrator Agent invokes the appropriate specialist through Apex using ConnectApi, allowing each agent to focus exclusively on its own business domain. This architecture significantly improves maintainability by isolating business responsibilities while allowing additional agents to be introduced without redesigning the entire system.

Phase 3 · Conversational Slot-Filling

Warranty requests rarely arrive with complete information. Instead of rejecting incomplete requests, the agents identify missing business attributes and conduct natural conversations with customers to collect only the required information. Agentforce conversation memory enables the platform to continue gathering information across multiple email exchanges without repeatedly requesting previously supplied details.

Phase 4 · Warranty Validation & RAG

Business decisions are grounded using enterprise knowledge rather than relying exclusively on large language model reasoning. Prompt Templates retrieve relevant warranty policies, product documentation and reference material from Data Cloud using Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG). This enables the platform to provide responses based on approved enterprise knowledge while reducing hallucinations during customer interactions.

Phase 5 · Business Execution

Once all mandatory information has been collected and validated, deterministic Salesforce automation performs the required business operations. Depending on the scenario, the platform updates the Case, initiates replacement order workflows, prepares contextual draft emails and escalates conversations requiring human intervention. AI performs reasoning while Salesforce remains responsible for executing authoritative business processes.

Key Engineering Decisions

Multi-Agent Architecture

Separate AI agents were created for vendor and customer interactions to isolate business responsibilities and simplify future expansion.

Deterministic Routing

An orchestration layer determines the correct execution path before invoking specialist agents, ensuring consistent business behaviour.

Programmatic Agent Invocation

Agents are invoked through Apex and ConnectApi, providing full control over orchestration rather than relying exclusively on declarative execution.

Trust Layer & Guardrails

Enterprise governance is maintained by combining Agentforce reasoning with Salesforce security and deterministic business validation before executing operations.

Conversational Slot-Filling

Rather than failing on incomplete requests, the platform progressively gathers missing information through natural conversations while preserving conversation context.

Grounded Knowledge (RAG)

Product manuals, warranty policies and enterprise documentation are retrieved from Data Cloud to ensure responses remain grounded in approved business knowledge.

Engineering Challenges

Building a production AI platform involved considerably more than integrating a language model into Salesforce. One of the primary challenges was designing orchestration logic capable of coordinating multiple specialised agents while maintaining conversation context throughout long-running email exchanges. Another significant challenge involved balancing AI flexibility with deterministic business execution. Warranty validation, replacement orders and Salesforce updates could not rely solely on probabilistic reasoning, requiring clear boundaries between AI decision making and traditional Salesforce automation. Prompt engineering also required continuous refinement to ensure responses remained concise, context-aware and aligned with enterprise business rules while minimising hallucinations. Finally, maintaining extensibility was an important architectural goal. The solution was intentionally designed so that future specialist agents and business capabilities could be introduced without restructuring the overall platform.
